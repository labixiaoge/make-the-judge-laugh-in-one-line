## Entry

> I told my reflection we were doing great and it looked away — even my mirror has stopped pretending.

100 characters. One line, one tiny story with a punchline: a pep talk to the mirror that the mirror refuses to go along with. The setup ("we were doing great") raises an expectation of self-belief, the turn ("it looked away") breaks it, and the laugh lands on the last beat ("even my mirror has stopped pretending") — the one witness to your life has given up on the act before you have. No stock joke, no pun anyone has heard, nothing that needs explaining.
